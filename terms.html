Sovereign Vault is a record-keeping tool. It stores what you enter, organises it, and shows it back to you.

It can also prepare your record to be handed to someone else — a clinician, or an AI tool you choose to ask — by attaching context describing what the data is, where it came from, and how it should be read. That context is about the data. It does not contain an assessment, a conclusion, or an opinion about your health, and it is not sent anywhere: it is placed on your clipboard with your record, for you to paste.

It does not diagnose. It does not treat. It does not advise. It does not calculate or recommend a dose, flag a risk, interpret a lab result, or tell you what any of your data means. Where it displays a number, that number is one you entered or one your own device supplied.
