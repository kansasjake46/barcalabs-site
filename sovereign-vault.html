How it works

From setup to the full picture

  1. Set up in minutes

    Copy the app's setup prompt into the AI you already use, then paste the answer back. The app reads it on your phone and sets up your protocol for you to check. Or add compounds, doses and timing yourself, in your own numbers and your own words.

  2. Log without opening the app

    When a dose is due, press and hold the notification and tap Log all. The app stays closed, and the lock screen only shows a count, never what you take.

  3. Sync your wearables

    The app reads what your watch saves to Apple Health or Health Connect: sleep, resting heart rate, steps, and HRV if your device records it. Polar connects directly.

  4. See it on one timeline

    Your inputs and your body's response, side by side, so cause and effect stop living in separate apps.

  5. Take the record out

    Your doses, labs and wearable data, all at once. Copy it as Markdown, JSON or a short brief and paste it into the AI you already use. Or save a spreadsheet with one row per day and a tab for everything else, ready for your doctor. Exports aren't encrypted, so you decide where they go.
