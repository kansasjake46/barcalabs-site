How it works

Four steps to the full picture

  1. Log your protocol

    Compounds, doses, timing, and your lab results — entered once, in your own numbers and your own words. Nothing is rounded, normalised, or reinterpreted into someone else's format.

  2. Sync your wearables

    Whatever your watch writes into Apple Health or Health Connect, the app reads — sleep, resting heart rate, steps, and HRV where your device provides it. Polar connects directly.

  3. See it on one timeline

    Your inputs and your body's response, side by side, so cause and effect stop living in separate apps.

  4. Copy the record out

    Your intake history, your lab results, and the readings from Apple Health or a wearable you've connected — copied to your clipboard as JSON or Markdown. Paste it wherever you want it: your own notes, the AI you trust, or a clinician.
