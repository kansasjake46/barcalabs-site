Support

Locked out, moving phones, or something not working? Start here.

Sovereign Vault is made by one small company. Mail goes to a person, not a ticket system: support@barcalabs.com.

Forgot your PIN

Your recovery code gets you back in

  1. Tap “Forgot your PIN?” on the unlock screen

    Enter the 25-character recovery code you wrote down when you set up the app.

  2. Set a new PIN

    Your records are all still there. You can turn Face ID or fingerprint back on in Settings.

Lost the code as well? Then nobody can open the vault, including us. We hold no copy of your records, your PIN or your code. That's what keeps them private, and it's also why we can't rescue them.

If you can still get in but have lost your code, make a new one in Settings. The new code replaces the old one right away. A backup only opens with the code that was active when you made it, so make a fresh backup right after you get a new code.

Moving to a new phone

Bring your vault with a backup file

Your phone's own backup won't bring your vault back. This will:

  1. On your old phone, make a backup

    Data → App Settings → Back Up My Vault. Save the file to iCloud Drive or Google Drive, so it's there on the new phone. Keep your recovery code somewhere else, like a password manager.

  2. On your new phone, install Sovereign Vault and restore

    When the app asks you to create a PIN, tap “Restore from a backup file.” Pick the file (it ends in .svbackup) and enter your recovery code.

  3. Set a PIN for the new phone

    Your old PIN doesn't come across with a backup. Turn on Face ID or fingerprint afterwards in Settings.

Make a backup before you change phones. If the old phone is gone and you never made one, the vault can't be brought back. Not by you, and not by us.

What we can do

And what nobody can

We can

  • Answer questions about how the app works
  • Take a bug report and tell you what we're doing about it
  • Tell you exactly what the app stores and where

We can't

  • Open your vault or reset your PIN
  • See your records. We hold no copy of them.
  • Refund an App Store purchase. Only Apple can, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Things we know about

Worth checking before you write

Your watch can stop sending data without telling you

Sovereign Vault reads what Apple Health or Health Connect holds. If the app that writes to Health stops (a permission changed, an update reset something, a companion app signed out), the data just stops arriving, and nothing announces it. If a number looks stale, check the app that owns it first. Not every watch writes every metric, either. Some never send HRV to Apple Health, and we can't read a number that was never written.

A rare storage defect

The database engine the app is built on has a known flaw that can, rarely, lose a recently saved entry without warning. No fixed version exists yet. The security page explains it in full. It's one more reason to make a backup now and then.
